## The Paradox of Learning

Every field moves quickly, which creates a feeling of always falling behind. Chasing trends and real learning are not the same thing.

## Three Layers

**Layer 1: Tools** — Learn a framework or API. Fast payoff, fast decay.

**Layer 2: Principles** — Understand mechanisms and first principles. Slow to change, high transfer.

**Layer 3: Problems** — Learn to find problems worth solving. Hardest, and most valuable.

## My Principles

1. Spend 80% of time on principles, 20% on tools
2. For every new skill, write one note (Feynman technique)
3. Verify understanding with a small project — not tutorials alone

> The best learning is teaching your future self.
