## The invisible audience

When I started writing notes publicly, the audience I had in mind was **future me** — someone who would forget the context, the dead ends, the "aha" moments.

## Benefits I've found

- **Clarity** — writing forces you to structure fuzzy intuitions
- **Accountability** — public commitments are harder to abandon
- **Serendipity** — the right person finds your note at the right time

## A practical approach

Don't wait until you are an expert. Document while learning:

1. What problem am I trying to solve?
2. What did I try?
3. What worked, what didn't?
4. What would I do differently?

The rough notes are often more useful than polished tutorials.
